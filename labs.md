# For the lab that already loans a keyboard.

If your center demonstrates ergonomic or one-hand keyboards, you already know who on campus cannot look down at a board while they work.

Handex is an early one-handed mobile keyboard. The switches are placed on the finger actions, so the user does not lift a finger to a different key and does not look at the hand to aim. It is still a proof of concept: about 20 words per minute on the inventor’s own sentences, with inexpensive buttons. I am looking for an introduction to a user, not a budget line.
